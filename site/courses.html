What I look at

A junior-friendly course checklist

  • Junior rate — is there one, and is it a real discount or $5 off?
  • Forward tees — marked, mowed, and at a yardage a 12-year-old can reach greens from.
  • Walkability — can you carry 18 without a shuttle bus of hills?
  • Pace — a 5½-hour round is the fastest way to make a kid quit golf.
  • Practice facility — grass or mats, short game area, bunker access.
  • Welcome — how the starter and the shop treat a teenager who shows up alone.
