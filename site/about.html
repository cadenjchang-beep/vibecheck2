About

Hi — I'm Caden.

I'm 15, I play junior tournaments, and I started this site because most golf reviews are written for 45-year-olds with a 105 mph swing.

Why this site exists

Two years ago I saved up for a driver based on reviews I found online. Every one of them was glowing. The club was way too heavy for me, I lost about 15 yards, and I hit it for four months before admitting it was a mistake.

None of those reviews were lying, exactly. They just weren't written for someone my size, my speed, or my budget. Nobody mentioned shaft weight. Nobody mentioned that a junior might not be able to load a stiff shaft. Nobody said "if you're still growing, here's what to think about instead."

So that's the whole idea here: reviews for the person actually holding the club.

What I actually play

I've been playing since I was 7 and competing since I was 11. Right now I play about 60 rounds a year and 12 or so junior tournaments, mostly in Oregon and Washington. I walk almost every round, which is why bag weight and walkability show up in so many of my reviews.

My scoring average sits in the mid-to-high 70s on a good stretch and low 80s when my driver leaves. I'm telling you that so you can calibrate: I'm a decent junior player, not a prodigy, and the gear that works for me will probably work for you if you're somewhere near that.

What I'm not

I'm not a coach, a club fitter, or an equipment engineer, and I don't pretend to be. I don't have a launch monitor at home. I can't tell you your optimal spin rate. What I can tell you is what I noticed over a few months of actually using something — and I'll always be specific about how I know.

For swing help, get a coach. For a proper fitting, get fit. This site is for the "should I buy this / should I play here" decision.
