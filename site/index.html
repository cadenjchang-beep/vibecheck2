How the stars work

Same scale on every review

I don't hand out 5s to be nice, and a 3 isn't an insult — a lot of good gear lands there because it has a real trade-off.

5/5

Exceptional. I'd recommend it without hesitating.

4/5

Very good, with minor drawbacks.

3/5

Solid, does the job, but has real trade-offs.

2/5

Below average. Hard to recommend.

1/5

Skip it.

For parents & coaches

Buying for a junior golfer?

Three things I put in every gear review because they're the ones that actually decide whether something gets used:

  • SizingShaft length, grip size, height range
  • Swing speedWho it suits and who it doesn't
  • Growing roomHow long before it's outgrown

I'm a junior golfer, not a fitter or a coach. For anything about swing changes or injuries, talk to a PGA professional or a doctor — I stick to what I noticed using the thing.
