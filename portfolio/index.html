06FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours here? Send me a message and I'll get back to you.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features you need and whether you're selling online. After a free consultation you'll get a clear, fixed quote up front, with no surprise invoices later.

How long does a project take?

Most small-business sites launch in two to four weeks. Online stores and custom builds usually take a little longer. You'll get a timeline with your proposal and regular updates along the way.

Which platform is right for me?

Webflow is great for marketing sites you want to edit visually, Shopify for online stores, WordPress for content-heavy sites, and custom code when you need something bespoke. I'll recommend the best fit for how you'll run the site.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. I can refresh the design, rebuild it on a better platform, or improve the speed and SEO of the site you already have.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. Every site comes with a short walkthrough so you can edit text, images and products on your own. If you'd rather not, a maintenance plan has you covered.

Do I own the website when it's done?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the design, content and domain are all yours.
